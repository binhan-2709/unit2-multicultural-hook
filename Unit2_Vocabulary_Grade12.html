📍 Station 1 — Context Clues
Instruction: Look for contextual signals to determine the correct vocabulary choice. (custom = social behavior vs. costume = clothing).
🔒 Read the story above, then click "✅ I've read it — Unlock Station 1" to unlock the questions.
1. In Japan, it's normal for people to bow instead of shaking hands when they meet someone new — this is considered a traditional social ______ that most people follow without thinking twice.
Explanation: Bowing politely when greeting people is a repeated social behavior / tradition ➔ custom (phong tục, tập quán).
2. For the parade, every dancer wore a bright, hand-sewn outfit decorated with feathers and sequins, made specially just for this one performance. This outfit is best described as a ______.
Explanation: "A bright, hand-sewn outfit decorated with feathers" is a specific set of clothing worn for a performance ➔ costume (trang phục hoá trang/biểu diễn).
🧬 Station 2 — Word Formation
Instruction: Transform base words with correct suffixes: -ion (celebrate → celebration), -al (tradition → traditional).
1. Every year, our neighbourhood organises a big for the Lunar New Year, with food, music, and fireworks.
Explanation: After article "a big", a singular noun is required ➔ celebrate becomes celebration.
2. In our family, it's to visit grandparents on the first day of the new year.
Explanation: After "it's", an adjective is required ➔ tradition becomes traditional.
3. The whole village came together for the harvest ______, which included singing, dancing, and a huge shared meal.
Explanation: Following "the harvest", a noun naming the festive event is needed ➔ celebration.
4. Many families in Vietnam still follow customs during Tet, such as giving lucky money to children.
Explanation: Before plural noun "customs", an adjective modifier is needed ➔ tradition becomes traditional.
